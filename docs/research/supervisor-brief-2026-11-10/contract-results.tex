\texttt{wal\_replay}, lease-ahead and interrupt-driven writes unless stated.

\texttt{page\_cache} mode, not its stronger barrier. Each arm has one data
SSD: Native on the coordinator VM, distributed WAL on the remote owner VM.

acknowledgement class does not match those costs.
